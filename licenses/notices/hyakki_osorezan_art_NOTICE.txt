Hyakki - Osorezan terrain art (assets/art/tilesets/osorezan.png)

Copyright (c) 2026 Clayton K. N. Passos and the Hyakki project contributors.

This tileset is an original work of the Hyakki project. It was drawn pixel by pixel by
tools/gen_osorezan_art.py (Python standard library only, deterministic, PNG written with zlib),
using colors taken from the Ninja Adventure palette (CC0): pinwheels (kazaguruma), stone cairns,
sotoba boards, Jizo statues, an old stone lantern, dead trees, volcanic rocks, a weathered torii,
sulfur vents and the ash, sulfur, pebble and water ground tiles. No third-party images, tiles or
AI image models were used.
