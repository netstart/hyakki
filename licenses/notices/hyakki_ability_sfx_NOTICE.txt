Hyakki - procedural guardian ability sound effects (assets/audio/sfx/a_*.wav) and the
synthesized "Om" chant of the Master's Mantra (assets/audio/voice/goro_3.ogg)

Copyright (c) 2026 Clayton K. N. Passos and the Hyakki project contributors.

These sounds are original works of the Hyakki project. The a_*.wav effects were synthesized
from scratch by tools/gen_ability_sfx.py (Python standard library only: noise, filters,
oscillators, additive partials and envelopes with fixed random seeds). The "Om" chant was
synthesized by tools/gen_ability_voices.py (additive glottal source and formant filters,
numpy/scipy, fixed seeds). No third-party recordings, samples or AI audio models were used.
