AI disclosure (from the pack's README.md, "AI disclosure"): every image in the pack was generated
by the author with Google Gemini from written text prompts, then cut, cleaned, aligned and
assembled with scripts and by hand; the author states that no existing artwork was used as an
input, reference or img2img source.

Used in Hyakki (assets/art/bosses/*, assets/art/midbosses/*, assets/art/elites/*): yokai and boss
animations from characters/ (jp_mob_* and jp_boss_*, "move" strips) for the stage bosses, the
mid-bosses (guardians) and the altar Elites. Each strip is reduced to the game's size (box filter,
binary alpha, colors snapped back to the character's own palette) and gets a 38x38 portrait
(Faceset.png) cut from the resting frame, by tools/import_sengoku.py. No pixels were redrawn.
