Copyright (c) 2026 Clayton K. N. Passos and the Hyakki project contributors.

These sprites are original works of the Hyakki project. They were drawn pixel by pixel by
tools/gen_portal_fx.py (Python standard library only, deterministic math, PNG written with zlib),
using colors taken from the Ninja Adventure palette (CC0). No third-party images, tiles or AI image
models were used.
