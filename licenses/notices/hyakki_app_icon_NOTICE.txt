Hyakki - app icon (assets/art/ui/app_icon/*)

Copyright (c) 2026 Clayton K. N. Passos and the Hyakki project contributors.

The Android launcher icons (legacy 192x192 and adaptive foreground, background and
monochrome layers, 432x432) are generated by tools/make_store_art.gd (Godot script,
deterministic). The night sky, stars, moon, hills, glow and paper lantern are original
pixel art drawn by that script. The guardian's face is Aki's Faceset from the
"Ninja Adventure - Asset Pack" by pixel-boy & AAA (CC0 1.0), already shipped under
assets/art/actors/heroes/aki/, with its flat background removed and a 1 px outline added,
scaled with nearest-neighbor integer scaling.
No AI image models were used.
