Hyakki - skill icons (assets/art/ui/skill_icons/**)

Copyright (c) 2026 Clayton K. N. Passos and the Hyakki project contributors.

These 24x24 pixel-art icons (weapons, evolutions, unions, passives, sanctuary upgrades
and Kami) are generated by tools/gen_skill_icons.gd (Godot script, deterministic).
Frames, gradient backgrounds and most silhouettes are original pixel art drawn in that
script. Some silhouettes reuse item sprites from the "Ninja Adventure - Asset Pack" by
pixel-boy & AAA (CC0 1.0), already shipped under assets/art/items/.
No AI image models were used.
